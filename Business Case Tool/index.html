Business Case Tool

Overview

Small and medium-sized businesses looking to invest in IT or automation often struggle to make a well-founded decision: the financial implications are complex, the variables are many, and there is rarely a structured way to compare options. The Business Case Tool was developed for the Brabant Development Agency (BOM) and Smart Industry to address exactly that gap — giving businesses a clear, quantitative foundation for their IT and automation investment decisions.

The application allows users to create and manage business cases through an intuitive interface. By entering a set of project parameters, the tool evaluates financial viability across multiple indicators — including payback period and Net Present Value (NPV) — and presents the results both in structured tables and interactive charts. A convenient dashboard export makes it easy to share findings with stakeholders. Beyond a single scenario, the tool supports side-by-side scenario comparisons, letting users tweak individual parameters to stress-test assumptions and fine-tune their business case before committing to an investment.

Collaboration

The requirements for the application were shaped in close collaboration with experts at TNO (the Dutch national innovation and applied-science consultancy), combining theoretical financial modelling knowledge with practical experience from the field. This cross-domain sparring ensured that the tool's analytical framework was both methodologically sound and directly usable by the businesses it was designed for.

Project Phase

The tool was delivered as a production application, actively used by businesses engaging with BOM and Smart Industry to evaluate IT and automation investments. The structured feedback cycle ensured the tool evolved continuously based on real user needs, moving beyond an initial release into a maintained product with an ongoing improvement loop.
