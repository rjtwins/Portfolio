ASML ESRS Reporting with scenario analysis

Overview

ASML's Environment, Energy & Greenhouse gas (EEG) team needed a structured way to model their emissions and to explore the impact of different operational and policy choices. The goal of this project was to give EEG experts a clear, quantitative picture of their current emissions footprint and to provide them with concrete scenario analysis options — so that they could compare trajectories, test assumptions, and support data-driven sustainability decisions.

Collaboration

The model was built in close collaboration with specialists across three domains inside ASML:

  • EEG team — domain expertise on emission sources, measurement methods, and regulatory requirements (ESRS).
  • Sustainability team — strategic context and long-term reduction targets.
  • Business Intelligence team — data availability, data quality, and integration with existing reporting pipelines.

Working across these teams ensured that the model reflected both the technical reality of the emissions data and the strategic questions that decision-makers actually needed answered.

My Role

I was responsible for the design and full implementation of the emission models using Lumina Analytica, a probabilistic modelling tool well suited for influence-diagram based scenario analysis. My contributions included:

  • Translating expert knowledge and data requirements into a structured model architecture.
  • Building and validating the full quantitative model in Analytica, covering multiple emission scopes and configurable levers.
  • Designing the scenario framework so that experts could interactively adjust key parameters and immediately observe the downstream impact on reported totals.
  • Ensuring the model output aligned with ESRS reporting standards.

Project Phase

This project served as the prototype and modelling phase of a broader emissions reporting initiative at ASML. The resulting model demonstrated the feasibility of quantitative scenario analysis for sustainability reporting and laid the groundwork for further tooling and data integration efforts.
