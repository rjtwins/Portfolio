ASML Analytica – CO₂ Emissions Modeling & Scenario Analysis

Duration: ~6 months

Context

Developed a prototype emissions intelligence system to model and analyze CO₂ emissions across business operations. The project aligned with EU ESRS regulatory requirements and served as a proof of concept for a future internal tool.

Worked within a multi-stakeholder environment involving domain experts in CO₂ emissions, distributed data owners across departments, and C-level stakeholders.

Challenge

Emissions data was fragmented across multiple departments, with inconsistencies in definitions, units, and levels of detail. There was no unified structure to represent emissions or evaluate reduction strategies.

The core challenge was to reconcile these differences into a coherent, technically sound model while aligning stakeholders on shared standards.

Solution

Designed and implemented a comprehensive influence diagram using Lumina Analytica to model emissions across sources, measurement methods, and regulatory classifications.

  • Unified heterogeneous datasets into a consistent multi-dimensional model
  • Engineered mappings between incompatible data structures and units
  • Enabled scenario analysis for evaluating CO₂ reduction measures
  • Defined data flow architecture, including sourcing and transformation
  • Established data integrity and normalization standards

Impact

  • Delivered the first unified view of emissions across business domains
  • Enabled C-level stakeholders to explore emissions drivers and reduction scenarios
  • Identified key data gaps and inconsistencies, informing future data governance
  • Established a foundation for development of an internal emissions management tool
  • Shifted reporting from static compliance toward strategic decision support

Role

Sole technical implementer responsible for model development, system design, and data architecture.

  • Translated domain knowledge into a functional modeling system
  • Advised stakeholders on technical feasibility and data structuring
  • Aligned cross-functional contributors on a unified modeling approach
