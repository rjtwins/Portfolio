InlineStars

InlineStars is a 4X space strategy game that runs entirely in your terminal — no graphics engine, no GPU, just pure ASCII rendered in the console. Built in Rust using Ratatui, it takes the classic top-down 2D space strategy formula and squeezes it into a crisp, keyboard-driven interface.

Explore a procedurally generated galaxy, expand your empire system by system, exploit resources across star clusters, and exterminate rival factions through diplomacy or firepower. A deep research tree lets you unlock new technologies that reshape your strategic options over the course of a campaign.

At the heart of InlineStars is ship design. Every vessel in your fleet is built from the ground up — choose hull classes, stack weapons, engines, shields, and support modules to create ships tailored to your playstyle. A fast interceptor swarm, a long-range bombardment dreadnought, a carrier bristling with fighters: the configuration space is wide open.
